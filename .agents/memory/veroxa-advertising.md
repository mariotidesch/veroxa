---
name: Veroxa advertising imagery
description: User's visual direction for Veroxa promotional films.
---

For Veroxa advertising films, use imagery consistent with computing and digital services, not physical shops.

**Why:** The user rejected the physical-shop imagery in the initial film and explicitly requested “immagini coerenti al informatica. digitale non negozi fisici”.

**How to apply:** When creating or revising Veroxa promotional videos, explain the digital services through technology-focused imagery. This does not change the website's target customers.
